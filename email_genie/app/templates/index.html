🧞 Enron Email Explorer 🧞

🌟 Welcome to the Enron Email Explorer! 🌟

Curious about the emails behind Enron's infamous collapse?
This app lets you search and explore the Enron email dataset!
Here is how to use it:

  1. Type your search query – Enter a keyword, phrase, or term you want to find in the emails.
  2. Choose how many results to see – Pick the number of top emails to display (default= 5).
  3. Click “Search” – Emails matching your query will appear below with:
    - Subject: The email’s subject line.
    - Summary: A quick snapshot of the email content.
    - Body: The full email text, formatted like an Outlook message for easy reading.

Not sure what to search? Here are some suggested search phrases: “bankruptcy”, “audit failure”, “Chapter 11”, “financial collapse”, “insolvency”.
